About Me

I’m a CSE-AI student who is curious about technology, business, creativity, and building things from scratch. I enjoy learning how things work rather than simply memorizing them, and I’m especially interested in turning what I learn into real projects.

I’m currently developing my skills in computer science, artificial intelligence, programming, and full-stack development. I’ve been working with technologies such as PHP, Laravel, C++, databases, Git, and GitHub, and I’m gradually building a stronger foundation in software development.

I prefer practical learning — learning something and then actually using it in a project.

Alongside technology, I have a strong interest in business and entrepreneurship. I like exploring how businesses work, how products can be created and sold, how customers think, and how a small idea can eventually become something much bigger.

I’m not interested in only being a person who knows how to code; I want to understand how to build, operate, improve, and grow real businesses and products.

One of the real-world businesses I’ve been involved with is Ongoing Foods, a tiffin/food service. Working on it has given me experience beyond technology — including menus, pricing, customer communication, reviews, marketing, branding, Instagram content, WhatsApp communication, food presentation, and customer service.

It has taught me that running a business involves many small details that aren't visible from the outside.

I’m also very interested in design and content creation. I enjoy creating posters, advertisements, social-media content, branding ideas, and visual concepts. I often pay attention to small details and like improving something repeatedly until it looks and feels right.

I’m someone who likes to experiment. I’ve explored ideas involving products, online businesses, content creation, software projects, robotics, and different ways of combining technology with entrepreneurship.

Some ideas work, some don't, but I see each experiment as part of learning.

I also care about building a portfolio of things I have actually made, rather than having only certificates or theoretical knowledge. That’s why I’m becoming more interested in GitHub, projects, and documenting my progress.
